# CareOps Compensation & Benefits Policy
Version 3.0 | Effective Date: January 1, 2025

## Salary Bands
- Administrative Staff: $35,000 – $65,000 annually
- Registered Nurses: $68,000 – $95,000 annually
- Senior Nurses / Charge Nurses: $95,000 – $115,000 annually
- Physicians: $180,000 – $320,000 annually

## Health Insurance
Coverage begins on the first day of the month following 30 days of employment. CareOps covers 80% of premium for employee, 60% for dependents. Plan options: HMO, PPO, HDHP with HSA.

## Retirement Plan
401(k) with 4% employer match. Vesting schedule: 25% per year, fully vested at 4 years. Enrollment opens on hire date.

## Bonuses
Annual performance bonus: up to 8% of base salary for clinical staff, up to 6% for administrative staff. Paid in March.
Referral bonus: $2,000 for successful clinical hire referrals.

## Maternity & Paternity Benefits
Primary caregiver: 12 weeks paid at 100% base salary.
Secondary caregiver: 4 weeks paid at 100% base salary.
Additional 12 weeks unpaid FMLA available after paid leave.
