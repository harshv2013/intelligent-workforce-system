# CareOps Onboarding & Credentialing Guide — Clinical Staff
Version 4.1 | Effective Date: January 1, 2025

## Required Documents Before First Shift
1. Government-issued photo ID (passport or driver's license)
2. Current nursing license / medical license (state-specific)
3. DEA registration (physicians only)
4. BLS/ACLS certification (must be valid, not expired)
5. Malpractice insurance certificate
6. Completed background check authorization form
7. I-9 employment eligibility documentation
8. Direct deposit banking information
9. Emergency contact form
10. Signed confidentiality and HIPAA agreement

## Credentialing Timeline
Week 1: Document submission via HR portal
Week 2: Primary source verification (licenses, certifications)
Week 3: Background check processing
Week 4: Credentialing committee review and approval
Target: Full credentialing complete within 21 days.
Expedited credentialing available for critical need positions (7 days).

## Mandatory Training (First 30 Days)
- HIPAA Privacy & Security Training (online, 2 hours)
- CareOps EMR System Training (in-person, 8 hours)
- Fire Safety & Emergency Procedures (online, 1 hour)
- Infection Control & Prevention (online, 1.5 hours)
- Workplace Violence Prevention (online, 1 hour)

## License Expiry Management
HR receives automated alerts at 90, 60, and 30 days before expiry.
Employees must submit renewal documentation 30 days before expiry.
Working with expired credentials: immediate suspension pending review.
