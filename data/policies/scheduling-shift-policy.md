# CareOps Clinical Staff Scheduling & Shift Policy
Version 1.8 | Effective Date: January 1, 2025

## Standard Shifts
Day shift:    07:00 – 19:00 (12 hours)
Night shift:  19:00 – 07:00 (12 hours)
Standard rotation: 3 days on, 4 days off (alternating).

## Overtime
Any hours beyond 36/week are paid at 1.5x base hourly rate.
Double time applies on federal holidays.
Mandatory overtime: maximum 4 hours per shift, once per week.

## Night Differential
Night shift premium: $4.50/hour additional for hours 19:00–07:00.
Weekend premium: $3.00/hour additional Saturday and Sunday.

## Shift Swapping
Shift swaps permitted with 48 hours notice via scheduling portal.
Both employees must be same role/certification level.
Manager approval required. Maximum 3 swaps per month per employee.

## On-Call Requirements
ICU and ER staff: 2 on-call shifts per month mandatory.
On-call pay: $8/hour while on-call, full shift rate if called in.

## Understaffing Protocol
If unit is understaffed by >20%: charge nurse may mandate 4-hour extensions. Employees may refuse maximum twice per quarter.
