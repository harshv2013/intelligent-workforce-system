# CareOps Hospital Network — Employee Handbook
Version 2.1 | Effective Date: January 1, 2025

## Code of Conduct
All CareOps employees are expected to maintain professional behavior at all times. Violations are subject to a 3-stage disciplinary process: verbal warning, written warning, termination.

## Grievance Redressal
Employees may raise grievances via the HR portal within 30 days of the incident. HR will respond within 10 business days. Unresolved grievances escalate to the Employee Relations team.

## Workplace Behavior
Zero tolerance for harassment, discrimination, or workplace violence. All incidents must be reported to HR within 24 hours. Retaliation against reporters is grounds for immediate termination.

## Disciplinary Process
Stage 1: Verbal warning — documented in employee file
Stage 2: Written warning — signed by employee and manager
Stage 3: Termination — requires HR Director approval
Performance improvement plans (PIPs) run parallel to Stage 1/2.
