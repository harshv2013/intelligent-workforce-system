# CareOps Leave & PTO Policy
Version 2.3 | Effective Date: January 1, 2025

## PTO Accrual
Years 0–2: 15 days per year (1.25 days/month)
Years 3–5: 20 days per year (1.67 days/month)
Years 6+:  25 days per year (2.08 days/month)
PTO accrual begins on day one. Maximum carryover: 10 days.

## Sick Leave
8 days per year, non-accruing, resets January 1.
Sick leave beyond 3 consecutive days requires a doctor's note.
Sick leave cannot be used for scheduled PTO.

## Emergency Leave
Up to 3 days paid emergency leave per year for immediate family emergencies. Requires manager approval within 24 hours.

## Maternity / Paternity Leave
Refer to Compensation & Benefits Policy for full details.
FMLA eligibility: 12 months of employment + 1,250 hours worked.

## Leave Request Process
Submit via HR portal minimum 2 weeks in advance for planned leave.
Emergency leave: notify manager by phone before shift start.
Unapproved absences after 3 consecutive days: disciplinary action.
